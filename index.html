Our Story

From Perak's coconut groves, to your straw.

cocoqi is produced by Iringan Inspirasi Sdn Bhd in Teluk Intan, Perak — a region known for its coconuts. Every pouch of Sedap Gila is filled with real coconut water and a whisper of aromatic pandan, harvested and packed close to source so it reaches you tasting like it just fell off the tree.

No shortcuts, no preservatives, no added sugar. Just coconut water the way it's meant to be — refreshingly honest, and a little bit playful.
